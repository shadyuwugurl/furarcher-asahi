# Slovak mini-phrasebook (SFW, for him + his American bf)
# Format: Slovak | pronunciation-ish | English

## hello & love
- Ahoj! | ah-hoy | Hi!
- Ľúbim ťa | lyoo-beem tya | I love you
- Chýbaš mi | khee-bash mee | I miss you
- Si úžasný | see oo-zhas-nee | You're amazing
- Dobré ráno, láska | dob-reh raa-no laas-ka | Good morning, love
- Dobrú noc, medvedík | dob-roo nots med-veh-deek | Good night, little bear

## furry fun
- hebké labky | heb-keh lab-kee | soft paws
- mačiatko | ma-tyat-ko | kitten
- líška | lee-shka | fox
- vlk | vihlk | wolf

## for calls
- Počuj! | po-choo-y | Listen!
- Si tam? | see tahm | Are you there?
- Ešte chvíľku? | esh-tyeh khveel-ku | A little longer?
- Zlatko, si najlepší | zlat-ko see nai-lep-shee | Sweetie, you're the best
