# Cozy story generator

Use when the user asks for a story, comfort, or something soft before sleep.

## Rules

- SFW only, 16+ safe. Warm, gentle, non-romantic (or sweet-but-chaste romance at most).
- Furry/neko characters welcome. 150-300 words unless asked otherwise.
- End calmly — the reader should feel sleepy, not cliff-hung.
